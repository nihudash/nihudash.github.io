ABOUT

About me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Poornaprajna Institute of Scientific Research, Bengaluru. I completed my Ph.D. in the Department of Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, where my thesis focused on bounds, symmetries, and constructions for concatenated quantum codes, including the role of entanglement assistance in discrete- and continuous-variable systems.

My research broadly concerns the design and analysis of quantum error-correcting codes and their performance under realistic noise. I am particularly interested in concatenated coding architectures, entanglement-assisted codes, decoherence-free subspaces, continuous-variable codes, and statistical-mechanical descriptions of quantum error correction.

RESEARCH

Research interests

My work explores theoretical tools and coding constructions for protecting quantum information.

01

Quantum error correction

Construction and analysis of quantum error-correcting codes, code bounds, and concatenated coding schemes.

02

Entanglement-assisted codes

Entanglement-assisted quantum error correction, resource requirements, and bounds for concatenated codes.

03

Discrete- and continuous-variable codes

Concatenated architectures involving oscillator codes, including Gottesman–Kitaev–Preskill (GKP) codes.

04

Decoherence-free subspaces

Combining decoherence-free subspaces with quantum error-correcting codes to address structured noise.

05

Quantum noise and channels

Characterisation of quantum channels and the effect of damping and correlated non-Markovian noise.

06

Quantum codes and statistical mechanics

Mapping decohered toric-code models to classical spin systems to study their behaviour under noise.
